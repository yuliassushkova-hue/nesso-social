How Nesso tests a window with a phone.

The viewing check measures 30 seconds of sound with the window open, then 30 seconds with it shut, without moving the phone. It grades the difference, not the noise level: a phone microphone's gain is unknown, and in a difference it cancels out.

If shutting it takes off 20 dB or more, the room goes clearly quieter. Under 10 dB, the window keeps little of the street out.

One of 82 checks in Nesso's property viewing checklist.
