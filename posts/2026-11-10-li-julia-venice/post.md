In September we had to answer one question about Venice.

Can a flat in the historic city still be let to tourists for more than 120 days a year?

Most articles said no. The limit, they wrote, was approved on 24 October 2024.

So we read the city's own records.

The date was real. It was the day the city's executive approved a proposal. A change like that still needs the city council, and a council committee deferred it in November 2025. By mid-September we had found no vote after that.

The rule everyone described was still a draft.

Ten years in litigation left me with one habit. A summary of a law is not the law.

There was a second trap on the way. Two Italian decrees share the number 50, and our first citation had mixed them up. Opening the decree itself caught it.

In Nesso, short lets in Venice still read "not verified".

Until the council votes, that is the accurate answer.
