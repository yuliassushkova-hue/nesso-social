Buying your first home in Italy: what do you pay on top of the price? Swipe.

The example is the app's own: €250,000 from a private seller, first home, agency at 3%, a €175,000 mortgage. Taxes €5,100, agency €9,150 with VAT, tax on the mortgage €437.50: €14,687.50 on top of the price. Add the rendita catastale (the official rental value on the land registry) and the registration tax (imposta di registro) usually goes down.

Notary, bank and insurance come apart: no law sets them, so you need written quotes.
