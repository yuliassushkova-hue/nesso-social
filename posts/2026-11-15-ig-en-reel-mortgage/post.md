Before a home loan in Italy, the bank owes you the PIES, the sheet with every cost of the mortgage: free and made for you, before you're bound. Ask several banks and compare the TAEG, the Italian APR.

You get at least seven days to weigh a binding offer, you can move the mortgage to another bank at no cost and pay it off early with no penalty.
