In September we read fifty research findings about getting a home ready for viewings.

We sorted them by one question. Did anyone find the effect in a price?

The coffee one did not survive.

Scent does lift people's mood in shops. No study we found shows a home selling for more because of it. Classical music went the same way, and so did staging, in the one experiment we found.

The obvious tips are about how a flat feels on the day.

What did show up in prices was mostly about the flat itself: energy class, daylight, quiet rooms, a terrace.

In Bolzano, homes in energy class A sold for over 6% more than class G. That is a link in prices, not proof that a renovation adds it.

Coffee can stay. I would just put it at the bottom of the list.
