A 5-minute walk on a map can be 11 minutes on foot.

Before putting walk times on Nesso's maps, we measured how wrong a straight line is. Across 674 hexagons in Torino, the real walking route was 1.39 times longer at the median, and more than double for 13% of them. The gaps cluster where the Po, the Dora and the railway lines cut the city.

So every walk time in Nesso is routed over OpenStreetMap's street network, footpaths and steps included.
