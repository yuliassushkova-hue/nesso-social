We built an AI that is not allowed to say "looks fine".

In Nesso you can send a photo from a viewing. A model reads it and lists only what is visibly there: a stain, peeling, a crack, a gap, corrosion. Each comes with a question to ask about that part of the room.

It never reports an absence and gives no confidence score. "No mould" about a wall nobody has seen behind is how people stop looking.
