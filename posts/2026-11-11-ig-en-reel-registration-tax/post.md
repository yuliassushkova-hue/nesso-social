Buy from a private seller as a private person, and the registration tax (imposta di registro) on a first home is 2% of the taxable base (9% on other homes). The base can be the cadastral value (valore catastale): the rendita catastale, the official rental value on the land registry, × 1.05 × 110. You ask for it in the deed.

With a rendita of €800: €1,848 instead of €6,000 on a €300,000 price.

Run it with your own rendita: try Nesso, link in bio. DPR 131/1986, L. 266/2005. It's a calculation, not advice.
