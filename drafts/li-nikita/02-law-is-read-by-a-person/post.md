We build Nesso with AI coding agents, and they write much of our code. One job they never get: reading the law.

Short-let rules decide what a flat can earn, so our investment map shows them city by city. When we checked Venice, nearly every secondary source said a 120-day cap had been in force since October 2024. The council's own records say something else: it is a proposal (proposta di deliberazione 2024/1061), the commission deferred it in November 2025, and we found no final vote. So Venice reads "not verified" on our map, and no short-let figure is shown there.

In the same round of checks, an AI summary of a Ligurian regional law described an article the law does not contain.

Our rule since then: a city's status rests on the text of the act, read by a person, and it stays unsigned until someone has.
