A viewing often lasts twenty minutes. In that time people decide where they will live for years, holding a listing the seller wrote, next to an agent who is paid when the deal closes.

I'm one of the two founders of Nesso. We're building the other side of that conversation: a second opinion on a home before you sign.

What's live today:
- a ten-minute walk through the flat, picked from 82 checks for what matters to you
- every neighbourhood in 15 cities scored on walk times to shops, schools and parks
- published rent and sale figures where they exist, with the source next to each one
- what buying costs on top of the price

It runs in Italy and Denmark. Here I'll write about how it's built, what the data shows, and what we got wrong on the way.
