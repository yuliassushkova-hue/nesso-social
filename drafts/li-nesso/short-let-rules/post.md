We read the short-let rules of 10 Italian cities. The acts, not the articles about them. 📜

⛔ Restricted: Firenze, Roma, Bologna
✅ Permitted: Torino, Milano, Genova, Palermo, Catania
❔ Not verified: Venezia, Napoli

"Restricted" means very different things. In Roma the limit reaches only flats created by converting or splitting units inside the UNESCO centre. In Bologna it is a 50 m² minimum in the historic city.

Where a rule is not verified, Nesso's investment map shows no short-let score.
