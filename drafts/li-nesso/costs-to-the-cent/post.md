Same €250,000 flat in Italy: €5,000 or €1,617 in registration tax. The difference is one number from the land registry. 🧮

Buy a home as a person from a private seller and you can ask for the tax to be worked out on the cadastral value instead of the price (prezzo-valore). For a first home with a cadastral income of €700, as an example, the base drops from €250,000 to €80,850.

Nesso's buying-costs calculator does this as soon as you type the rendita from the visura catastale. The agency fee, VAT on the fee and the mortgage substitute tax each sit on their own line.
